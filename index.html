Research

Core research directions

Our work combines mathematical signal models, statistical learning, and deployable AI systems.

Signal Processing

Theory and algorithms for acquiring, analyzing, enhancing, and interpreting signals in complex environments.

Machine Learning

Representation learning, deep generative models, robust inference, and self-supervised learning for real-world data.

Intelligent Systems

Adaptive systems that perceive, reason, communicate, and make reliable decisions under uncertainty.
