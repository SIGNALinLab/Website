01

Speech Enhancement for Noisy Environments

We propose a deep learning-based speech enhancement system using a convolutional recurrent network with attention. The model suppresses stationary and non-stationary noise while preserving speech quality and intelligibility.

02

Deep Learning for ECG Arrhythmia Detection

We develop a 1D CNN–ResNet model that classifies ECG signals into multiple arrhythmia categories. The system is trained on ECG datasets and achieves strong performance across imbalanced arrhythmia classes.

03

Radar-Based Human Activity Recognition

We design an end-to-end radar sensing pipeline using FMCW radar data and a transformer-based model to recognize daily human activities with high accuracy and robustness to lighting conditions and occlusions.
